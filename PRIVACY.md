# Privacy Policy — GitHub LaTeX Renderer

Last updated: 2026-09-15

GitHub LaTeX Renderer does **not** collect, transmit, store, or share any
personal data or browsing data.

- The extension runs entirely locally in your browser.
- All rendering is performed by the bundled KaTeX library; the extension makes
  **no network requests** of any kind.
- The only data stored is your on/off toggle preference, saved locally via
  `chrome.storage.sync` (and synced by your own browser account if you have
  browser sync enabled).
- The extension reads page content on `github.com` solely to find and render
  LaTeX math notation in diff views. That content never leaves your browser.

If you have questions, open an issue at
https://github.com/ToastCheng/github-latex-renderer/issues
